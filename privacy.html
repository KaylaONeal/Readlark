Privacy policy

Readlark collects no data. Your documents stay on your device.

Updated October 27, 2026

Your documents

Text you paste and files you import are stored only inside Readlark on your device. Natural voices are generated by an AI model that runs on your device; your text is never sent to a server for speech generation.

Network access

Readlark connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

  • Web articles. When you add a web address, Readlark downloads that page directly from its website and keeps only the article text. The website sees an ordinary request from your device.
  • Voice model download. When you choose to download natural voices, Readlark downloads the model files (about 120 MB) from GitHub and verifies them with a fixed checksum. No personal information is sent.

Accounts, analytics and advertising

Readlark has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Purchases

Readlark Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; Readlark only receives Apple's confirmation that the purchase is valid, on your device.

Exporting and deleting your data

You can export your whole library as a JSON file in Settings at any time. Deleting a document removes it from your device; deleting the app removes all of Readlark's data. You can remove the downloaded voice model in Settings.

Children

Readlark does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: open an issue.
