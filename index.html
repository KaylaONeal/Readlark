Listen to anything, in a natural voice that never leaves your device.

Readlark reads PDFs, ebooks, web articles and your own text aloud. Its AI voices run entirely on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, so your documents are never uploaded and listening works offline.

What you can open

  • PDF and EPUB (no DRM) ✓
  • Markdown, HTML, plain text ✓
  • Web articles by address ✓

How it works

  1. Add something to read

    Paste text, import a file, or add a web page. Readlark keeps just the article text.

  2. Pick a voice

    Natural voices download once (about 120 MB) and then run offline. On iPhone and iPad, system voices are also available.

  3. Listen

    Sentences highlight as they are read. Tap any sentence to jump there. Readlark remembers where you stopped.
