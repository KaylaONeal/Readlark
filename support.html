Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Open an issue and include your device, system version and what you tried.

How do I get natural voices?

Tap the voice button in the player, then “Download Natural Voices”. The one-time download is about 120 MB; after that, natural voices work offline.

Why can't I find system voices on Mac?

On macOS, Readlark uses natural voices only. Built-in system voices are available on iPhone and iPad.

Which files can I import?

PDF, EPUB without DRM, Markdown, HTML and plain text. Scanned PDFs without a text layer can't be read yet.

A web article imported badly. What can I do?

Some pages hide their text behind scripts or sign-ins. Copy the article text and use “Paste Text” instead.

What does Readlark Pro include?

Unlimited natural voices, every voice, and audio export (M4A). It is a single purchase for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with Family Sharing. There is no subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in Readlark and tap “Restore Purchases” while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I export my documents?

Settings › Your data › Export Library. You get a JSON file with every document and your reading positions. Export is free.
